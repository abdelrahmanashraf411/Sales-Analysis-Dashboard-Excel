# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

**An interactive sales dashboard built with Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, product quantities, regional trends, and salesperson performance.**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and shipping data for 2019–2020 using Microsoft Excel. It transforms raw transactional data into structured summaries and interactive visualizations to make sales performance easier to understand.

The dashboard provides an overview of key sales metrics and allows users to explore business performance across different categories, including salespeople, regions, and customers.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Data Preparation & Formula-Based Calculations
- PivotTables & PivotCharts
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboard Design
- KPI Reporting
- Business Data Analysis

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains **2,155 records** and 21 columns covering sales transactions and shipping information.

Key fields include:

- Customer
- Country and Region
- Salesperson
- Order ID and Order Date
- Product ID and Product Name
- Unit Price and Quantity
- Discount and Extended Price
- Shipping Dates and Freight

Additional calculated fields, such as `Order Month` and `Order Year`, support time-based analysis.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes six charts designed to present key business metrics and sales insights.

### 1. Total Sales by Salesperson
Compares sales performance across salespeople to identify differences in individual contributions to total sales.

### 2. Total Quantity by Region
Visualizes monthly quantity trends across Europe, North America, and South America.

### 3. Total Sales
Displays an overall sales KPI to provide a quick overview of business performance.

### 4. Total Quantity
Summarizes the total quantity of products sold.

### 5. Top 8 Countries
Highlights leading countries based on sales and quantity metrics.

### 6. Top 10 Customers
Identifies key customers based on their contribution to sales and quantities.

## 🔍 Data Analysis Process

1. **Data Preparation:** Organized the sales and shipping dataset and used calculated fields to support analysis.
2. **Data Aggregation:** Created PivotTables to summarize sales, quantities, and order-related metrics.
3. **Data Visualization:** Built PivotCharts to compare sales performance across different business dimensions.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Designed a visual dashboard to present KPIs and sales trends in a clear format.
5. **Interactive Analysis:** Used Excel filtering features to explore the data and support comparisons.
